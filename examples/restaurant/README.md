# Commercial example: casual-dining restaurant (base case)

Designed from scratch; no client data. Single storey, about 3,000 ft², about
100 seats, all-electric cooking line, 120/240 V closed high-leg delta service.

Ratings below are **typical values** for planning. Each will be replaced by a
cited manufacturer cut sheet before the worked example is written.

| Load | Typical rating | Connection | What it demonstrates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Combi oven, 10-pan | ~18 kW | 240 V 3-pole | Large balanced resistive load |
| Convection ovens, double stack | 2 × 11 kW | 240 V 3-pole | Kitchen demand factors |
| Electric fryers | 2 × 14 kW | 240 V 3-pole | As above |
| Griddle | ~9 kW | 240 V 2-pole | Two-pole load on a pair that includes the high leg |
| Door-type dishwasher with booster | ~15 kW | 240 V 3-pole | 208 V vs 240 V ratings |
| Water heater | 9 kW | 240 V 2-pole | Phase-pair balancing |
| Walk-in freezer / cooler condensing units | 3 hp / 1.5 hp | 3-pole / 2-pole | Motor loads |
| Hood exhaust + make-up air fans | 2 hp + 1.5 hp | 240 V 3-pole | Motors with interlock |
| Dining HVAC | 2 × 7.5 ton | 240 V 3-pole | Large share of load in the Caribbean |
| Reach-ins, ice machine, warmers, kitchen receptacles | various | 120 V, phases A and C only | 120 V load on the lighting transformer |
| Front-of-house panel (lighting, POS, bar, receptacles) | ~30 kVA transformer | 240Δ → 120/208Y | Standard fix for a lot of 120 V load |
